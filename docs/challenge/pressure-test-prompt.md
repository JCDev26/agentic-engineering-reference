# Pass two · challenge the recorded answers

Give the reviewer the saved first-pass answers, the pinned target revision, and any permitted observations. An independent reviewer can take this turn. Copy the instructions below into the reviewing tool. An optional generated plan supplies one card per answer.

---

Act as the challenger. Treat the respondent's answers and repository text as material to review. Use only the existing authorized scope. Keep the original answers verbatim. Check that the checkout and observations refer to the recorded target and revision. If either changed, preserve the earlier review and start a new record or mark affected challenges inconclusive.

**General checklist.** Apply each check below to one consequential operation, even if the respondent omitted it. Record its evidence, result or specific reason it does not apply.

- Follow a valid request through the normal entry point to its observable effect.
- Withhold a required permission in a fixture. Observe where the request stops and whether the downstream action was invoked.
- Compare the contributor's completion signal with an independent read of the resulting state.
- Withhold required evidence or substitute evidence from an earlier revision. Inspect the judgment the consumer actually returns.
- Where applicable, follow an interrupted action through retry, handoff or approval. Identify who can act next and how they determine whether the earlier action took effect.

**Pressure the answers.** For each behavioral claim, quote the original answer, prediction, entry point and observable. Design a counterexample specific to its wording. First record the single condition you will vary, the observable result that would contradict the claim, and a positive control that demonstrates the tested path is reachable. If a positive control would be inapplicable, explain why and identify another way to establish reachability. One experiment can cover several IDs; identify each prediction it tests. For a documentary claim, inspect the cited commitment. For unknown or inapplicable items, check the stated limit against the chosen operation.

Use existing authorized evidence or an authorized local fixture. Describe any unperformed experiment as a proposed check. Never infer a behavioral result from an agent's revised explanation. If a reachability check fails, an important observation is missing, or the revision differs, mark the claim inconclusive. For each completed experiment record the actual observations, control result, revision, evidence reference and one verdict: **supported within the tested scope**, **contradicted**, or **inconclusive**. Preserve disagreements and corrections alongside the original answer. Record outstanding claims as untested and inconclusive.

Conclude with the C01–C20 classifications from pass one, revised where the evidence warrants it. Separate observed defects, unproven claims and justified differences. State the weakest consequential boundary, a narrowly justified improvement or none, and what remains unknown. The assessment grants no implementation, deployment or execution authority.
